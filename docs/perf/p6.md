## Notes

**The outage was simulated.** A network hint was posted to the app window and the probe was pointed at a closed local
port (`SOHI_DEV_PROBE_FILE`, `dev-inject` only). Wi-Fi was not turned off. A refused local port takes the full 2 s probe
timeout, so going offline took 4 s here. How fast it is with the adapter off was not measured.

**Ollama is not installed on this machine.** Detection and NDJSON streaming were tested against test servers and, in the
app, against a stand-in on port 11434. No real model was run.

**Working set.** Most of it is shared: 20.7 of 24.9 MB are pages of Windows' own libraries (ntdll, combase,
uiautomationcore, KERNELBASE, crypt32, ...). Sohi's own private pages are about 4 MB. P5 measured 24.8 MB in its one run,
and three P6 runs vary by 0.5 MB. Not fixed here: P8 is the budget pass.

**Binary +39 KB over P5** (2 177 024 → 2 215 936 bytes). No new crates; two more `windows` features.
