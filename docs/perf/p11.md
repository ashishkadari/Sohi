# Perf report — P11 (sounds)

Measured 2026-11-24 on the dev laptop (Windows 11 26300) with a `dev-inject` test instance (sounds at -48 dB, injected
speech), one run.

| Metric | Measured | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| asked for → first samples handed to the device | 19, 20, 23, 28 ms (device awake) | 100 ms | pass |
| take-over with its sound, frame p95 | 11.7 ms (worst 13.3) | 16 ms | pass |
| way back with its sound, frame p95 | 13.3 ms (worst 16.2) | 16 ms | pass |
| CPU in 30 s, parked afterwards | 16 ms | 30 ms (60 ms per minute) | pass |
| memory then | private 6.4 MB, working set 30.7 MB | 20 MB; 27 MB is for voice turns only | |

Played once each in that run: mic opened, utterance ended, question asked (typed), cape over, cape back. The log shows it;
how they sound was not judged by anyone yet.

The sound of the mic opening, fed to the voice detector, is discarded at every volume (at most 130 ms voiced; an utterance
needs 250 ms). A room's echo is not in that test. The error and the reminder sound were not played live, nor any sound on
a device that was asleep (opening one took about 300 ms in P5).

## Full mode's sounds, again

The whoosh became a stuttering signal (`sound/glitch.rs`) after the runs above; those were not repeated. The share of
its energy, from the written samples: entering 57 % below 200 Hz, 38 % to 1 kHz, 5 % to 3 kHz, 0.8 % above; leaving
54 %, 35 %, 10 %, 0.9 %. The largest step between two samples is 0.05 and 0.07 of full scale, so no clicks.
How it sounds is for the user to say.
