## Notes

**Tool descriptions in the prompt do not show up in the latency.** The first question, 10 turns at a time, alternating: with
tools p50 1.82 s, without 1.54 s, with 1.42 s, without 1.58 s. The service varies more from one batch to the next than
the tools add.

**A turn with a tool asks the model twice.** 0.4–0.8 s more, plus what the tool takes. The slowest (3.32 s) was "open
Notepad": first use of the index and a 1.5 s wait for the second answer.

**Helper process.** `ShellExecuteW` and the apps folder first ran inside Sohi: the working set went to 43.6 and 44.5 MB
after `launch_app`. They now run in a short-lived helper (see the SPEC CHANGE in §5.6). Opening something takes about 250 ms
with it. Whether an opened window comes to the front was not checked.

**Working set.** Still at the limit since P5 (20.7 MB of it are Windows' own libraries). P8 is the budget pass.

**Binary +245 KB over P6** (2 215 936 → 2 461 184 bytes). No new crates; more `windows` features (clipboard, registry, job
objects, endpoint volume).
